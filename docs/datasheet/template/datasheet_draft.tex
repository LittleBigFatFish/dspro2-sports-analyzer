\noindent This sheet describes our main dataset, the Football Image Dataset. Five more datasets are compared in the table at the end.

% --- Section 1: Overview ---
\section{Dataset Overview}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Official Name:} Football Image Dataset (GitHub \texttt{skaczylo/Football-Image-Dataset}).
    \item \textbf{Owner / Contact:} GitHub user skaczylo; no other contact given.
    \item \textbf{Short Description:} 612 frames from football matches (512 train, 100 val) with 10,136 boxes for ball, player and referee in YOLO format. Frames come from TV broadcasts, highlight videos and one video game.
    \item \textbf{Version \& Date:} No release tag. Commit \texttt{2642128} (2026-06-23), cloned 2026-10-20.
    \item \textbf{Reference:} \url{https://github.com/skaczylo/Football-Image-Dataset} \newline BibTeX in the README, no paper.
\end{itemize}

% --- Section 2: Motivation ---
\section{Motivation and Intended Use}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Primary Purpose:} A ready-to-use training set for Ultralytics YOLO (README). Funding is not stated.
    \item \textbf{Intended Use:} Detecting players, referees and the ball. We test pretrained YOLOv8n, YOLO12n and YOLO26n at 640 and 1280\,px, later we fine-tune on it.
    \item \textbf{Out-of-Scope Use:} Identifying people; tracking or possession; claims about unseen matches; reporting video-game frames as real footage.
\end{itemize}

% --- Section 3: Composition ---
\section{Composition and Coverage}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Data Types:} Unstructured: JPEG images, one YOLO label file per image, \texttt{data.yaml} with the class names.
    \item \textbf{Population:} Professional matches 2024--25 from TV. Train: Bundesliga, Saudi league, J-League, Copa Libertadores, 2024 Community Shield. Val: three highlight clips and the video game eFootball PES 2021.
    \item \textbf{Dataset Size:} 612 images, 10,136 boxes (8,844 player, 792 referee, 500 ball). 548 images are 1920$\times$1080, the rest smaller.
    \item \textbf{Known Gaps / Imbalances:} 43 of the 100 val images are from the video game. Balls are tiny: 74 of 83 val balls are under 8\,px at 640\,px input. No rain or snow, no amateur or youth games, no wide tactical camera.
\end{itemize}

% --- Section 4: Collection ---
\section{Sources and Collection}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Provenance:} Public third-party footage: TV broadcasts, YouTube highlights and one video game. The number of matches is unknown.
    \item \textbf{Collection Method:} Frames cut from videos. Sampling rule and tools are not documented.
    \item \textbf{Jurisdictions:} Broadcasts from Germany, Saudi Arabia, Japan, South America, England and two international matches. Where the data was labelled is not stated.
\end{itemize}

% --- Section 5: Labels ---
\section{Labels and Targets}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Target Definition:} Boxes for 0 ball, 1 player (goalkeepers included), 2 referee (linesmen included). For pretrained COCO models, player and referee count as \emph{person}, ball as \emph{sports ball}.
    \item \textbf{Labeling Process:} ``Manually annotated'' per the README; annotators and tool unknown.
    \item \textbf{Quality Control:} None documented upstream. Ours: the audit below and a spot-check of 35 images by Johan \review{error rate}.
\end{itemize}

% --- Section 6: Data Quality Audit ---
\section{Quality Assessment (Data Audit)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Preprocessing Steps:} None; raw data is never edited. For inference, images are only resized to 640 or 1280\,px.
    \item \textbf{Initial Audit Findings:} 0 errors, 31 warnings, among them 11 boxes under 5\,px and one empty label on a frame full of players. 48 of the 100 val images are also in Soccana train, so a model trained on Soccana cannot be tested on this val split.
    \item \textbf{Quality Dimensions:} \emph{Completeness:} every image has a label file. \emph{Accuracy:} two label errors found, spot-check \review{result}. \emph{Consistency:} class IDs match \texttt{data.yaml}; real and video-game frames are mixed. \emph{Timeliness:} matches 2024--25; commit and file hashes recorded. Zero-shot at 1280\,px on real val: person AP50 .95--.97, ball recall .35--.52 ($n=46$ balls).
\end{itemize}

% --- Section 7: Ethics & Governance ---
\section{Legal, Ethical, and Governance}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Sensitive Data:} Players, officials and fans are identifiable; there are no identity labels. The data stays on our machines and is not in git.
    \item \textbf{Compliance:} EU AI Act: minimal risk, since we do not identify people and it is coursework \review{confirm with supervisor}. GDPR / nFADP: the people shown are personal data; teaching use only, stored locally, not shared.
    \item \textbf{Consent \& Licensing:} CC BY 4.0 per \texttt{LICENSE}. The frames come from broadcasts and a Konami game, so the licence covers the annotations at most. Consent is not mentioned.
\end{itemize}

% --- Section: Structure & Schema ---
\section{Dataset Structure and Schema}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{File Formats:} JPEG, YOLO \texttt{.txt}, \texttt{data.yaml}.
    \item \textbf{Directory Layout:} \texttt{images/} and \texttt{labels/}, each with \texttt{train/} and \texttt{val/}.
    \item \textbf{Schema Summary:} Image and label share the base name; one line per box: \texttt{class x\_center y\_center width height}.
    \item \textbf{Units \& Encodings:} Coordinates normalised to $[0,1]$ by image size; UTF-8 text.
\end{itemize}
